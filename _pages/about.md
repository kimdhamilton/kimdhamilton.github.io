My work centers on digital identity, privacy, and human agency. I’ve built credential systems that help people carry and share proof of their achievements.

Today, I’m focused on how AI agents can follow someone’s intent, stay within the permissions they’ve been given, and leave a clear record of what they did, even when they delegate work to other agents.

I’ve built core .NET libraries at Microsoft, distributed systems at Expedia, and digital credential infrastructure at MIT. As executive director of the Decentralized Identity Foundation, I worked with a global community to advance open identity standards.

### Advisory Work

- **Technology Committee Member**, [MOSIP](https://www.mosip.io/) — Open-source digital identity infrastructure
- **Architecture Advisor**, [ASU Trusted Learner Network](https://tln.asu.edu/who-we-are) — Interoperable credentials for education and work

### Previous Projects

#### Blockcerts

I co-created [Blockcerts](https://www.blockcerts.org/) with the MIT Media Lab and built open-source libraries for verifying digital credentials. As CTO of Learning Machine, I led engineering for products based on that work. Read more in [What We Learned from Designing an Academic Certificates System on the Blockchain](https://medium.com/mit-media-lab/what-we-learned-from-designing-an-academic-certificates-system-on-the-blockchain-34ba5874f196).

#### MIT Digital Credentials Consortium

As Lead Architect for MIT's [Digital Credentials Consortium](https://digitalcredentials.mit.edu/), I designed infrastructure enabling learners to control and share their academic achievements across institutions. Key outputs included the [Learner Credential Wallet Specification](https://digitalcredentials.mit.edu/docs/Learner-Credential-Wallet-Specification-May-2021.pdf) (with the US Department of Education) and [Building the Digital Credential Infrastructure for the Future](https://digitalcredentials.mit.edu/docs/white-paper-building-digital-credential-infrastructure-future.pdf).

#### Centre Consortium

At Centre Consortium, I led identity and standards work for USDC. I also coordinated security audits and release planning for its Ethereum Merge transition and helped improve the review process for new blockchain integrations.

#### Digital Identity Standards

I chaired the World Economic Forum's Digital Identity Technical Working Group, leading a multi-stakeholder effort that produced the [Reimagining Digital ID](https://www3.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Reimagining_Digital_ID_2023.pdf) framework.

### Research & Work

My recent work explores digital identity as it relates to challenges and opportunities introduced by AI. Recent publications in this area include:

- [Personhood Credentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07892): Privacy-preserving tools to distinguish authentic humans online
- [Chains of Trust](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/59762/chapter-abstract/508609059?redirectedFrom=fulltext): Frameworks to combat synthetic data risks in AI systems (Oxford Intersections, 2025)
- [Decentralized Identity of Artifacts](https://doi.org/10.1109/IS61756.2024.10705248): Methods to verify the authenticity of digital artifacts (IEEE, 2024)

Much of my work centers on decentralized identity standards and open-source development, applying these technologies across domains—including education and workforce systems—to enable individuals to control their data. Key contributions include:

- [Decentralized Identity: Where Did It Come From and Where Is It Going?](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCOMSTD.2019.9031542), IEEE Communications Standards Magazine
- [Building the Digital Credential Infrastructure for the Future](https://digitalcredentials.mit.edu/docs/white-paper-building-digital-credential-infrastructure-future.pdf), MIT
- [Applying Self-Sovereign Identity Principles to Interoperable Learning Records](https://www.t3networkhub.org/resources/applying-self-sovereign-identity-principles-to-interoperable-learning-records-principles-challenges-and-community-guidance-v2), US Chamber of Commerce Foundation

I co-wrote [*Learning UML 2.0*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/learning-uml-2-0/0596009828/).

See my [publications page](/publications/) for selected research and standards work.
